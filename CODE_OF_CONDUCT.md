# Code of conduct

This project is a place for mechanics, fleet owners and hobbyists to help each other get trucks
diagnosed. Please keep it friendly and useful.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient – many people here are not programmers, and English is not everyone's
  first language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on the problem, not the person.
- Share logs and findings in the anonymised form (see "Send to developer" in the README).
- Put safety first: do not encourage anyone to disable brakes, speed limits, seat/operator detection
  or other safety functions.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information (names, addresses, truck serial numbers, SIM/IMEI
  data, company data) without their permission.
- Requests for or links to cracked/unlicensed Linde software, licence keys or Linde's copyrighted
  files.
- Spam and off-topic advertising.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may
temporarily or permanently block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact the
maintainer ([@oskare2345](https://github.com/oskare2345)) through GitHub. Reports are handled
privately.
